Game Design Document
Game Title

Endless Brainrot Escape

Genre

3D Horror Comedy / Endless Chase

High Concept

Endless Brainrot Escape is a surreal third-person horror-comedy game set inside a school located in a Mordor-like dark environment. The player is constantly chased by a weird, funny, and disturbing brainrot-style creature. The goal is not to win, but to survive as long as possible while being followed through the corridors of the school in an endless and absurd chase.

Game Concept

The game takes place in a dark school environment surrounded by a volcanic Mordor-inspired landscape. The player controls a character who can move through the corridors of the building. A strange creature with a brainrot-inspired design follows the player everywhere, making unsettling but also ridiculous sound effects. The creature never stops chasing, and the player can never truly escape or hide. This creates a repetitive, meaningless, and uncomfortable gameplay loop, which is intentionally part of the game’s concept.

Story / Narrative

The player is trapped inside a strange school in a cursed land. The environment feels abandoned, oppressive, and surreal. A mysterious creature wanders the halls and locks onto the player’s presence. From that point on, the player is chased forever.

There is no real explanation, no ending, and no escape. The creature’s constant pursuit turns the school into a nightmarish loop. The experience is both humorous and disturbing, reflecting internet “brainrot” absurdity mixed with horror.

Gameplay Objective

The main objective is to survive for as long as possible while being chased by the enemy. The player can run through the school corridors, but cannot permanently escape. The game is intentionally endless.

Core Gameplay Loop

The player explores or moves through the school corridors.

The chasing creature detects the player.

The creature follows the player while making strange sound effects.

The player keeps running and changing direction.

The chase continues infinitely.

Player Mechanics

Third-person movement

Walking/running in four directions

Camera-controlled view

Emotional/animated character states

Endless escape behavior

Enemy Mechanics

The enemy constantly follows the player

The enemy has a strange and unsettling visual design

The enemy plays funny but creepy sound effects while chasing

The enemy cannot be defeated

The enemy creates constant pressure and absurd tension

Environment Design

The game takes place in a one-floor school building with connected corridors modeled in Unity using ProBuilder. The surrounding environment is inspired by Mordor:

dark rocky terrain

volcanic atmosphere

gloomy sky

red/orange lighting

oppressive mood

The scene also includes the Eye of Sauron, which watches the environment and tracks the mouse cursor in real time.

Visual Style

The visual style combines:

dark fantasy

surreal horror

absurd comedy

exaggerated and uncanny character design

The school itself is simple and maze-like, while the outside world feels hostile and otherworldly.

Audio Design

The audio is important for both tension and humor.
Planned audio elements:

looping chase sound effects

weird brainrot-like enemy noises

ambient dark wind or atmospheric background sounds

footsteps or movement sounds

Character Animation

The player character uses an Animation Blend Tree with the following animation states:

Idle

Sad

Happy

Dancing

These animations help make the character more expressive and contribute to the absurd tone of the game.

Controls

W / A / S / D: Move character

Mouse: Control camera direction

Additional keys may trigger emotional or joke animations

Technical Specifications

Engine: Unity

Modeling Tool: ProBuilder

Environment Tool: Terrain system

Skybox and fog used for atmosphere

Third-person character controller

Animator Controller with Blend Tree

Mouse-based Eye of Sauron tracking script

One-floor school layout with connected corridors

Target Platform

PC

Target Audience

This game is aimed at players who enjoy:

absurd internet humor

surreal horror

meme-inspired game concepts

short experimental experiences

Unique Selling Point

The game combines a dark fantasy school setting with endless horror pursuit and brainrot-style absurdity. Its main appeal comes from the contrast between comedy and discomfort: the enemy is ridiculous, but the constant chase still feels stressful and oppressive.

Scope

This is a small-scale prototype focused on:

environment design

animation system

atmosphere

basic AI chase idea

surreal horror-comedy tone

Conclusion

Endless Brainrot Escape is a simple experimental game prototype that turns a school corridor into an endless nightmare. The player is chased forever by a bizarre and terrifyingly funny creature, creating a looping experience that is intentionally stressful, strange, and meaningless.
